I am developer behind [SleepBreeze](https://sleepbreeze.eu/), a Bluetooth sunrise alarm lamp that simulates natural sunlight to help users wake up more gently. The lamp gradually increases its brightness and warmth before the set alarm time, creating a gentle wake-up experience.
