I had the opportunity to travel to Canada to attend ICSE 2025, the International Conference on Software Engineering.
This trip marked a milestone, full of exciting firsts:

- My first intercontinental travel,
- My first research conference,
- My first paper presentation at the Flaky Test Workshop *FTW 2025*, (See my slides [here]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/pdf/ICSE_2025_presentation.pdf))

In addition to presenting, I volunteered as a student helper throughout the week, helping ensure the conference ran smoothly.
It was a fantastic experience connecting with researchers, attending inspiring talks, and I'm looking forward to many more opportunities like this!
