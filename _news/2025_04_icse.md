I had the opportunity to travel to Canada to attend ICSE 2025, the International Conference on Software Engineering. 
This was a special milestone for me, with lots of first times:

- It was my first intercontinental travel,
- It was my first research conference,
- It was my first paper presentation at the Flaky Test Workshop (FTW 2025)!

In addition to that, I also volunteered as a student helper throughout the week to facilitate the smooth running of the conference.
It was a fantastic experience connecting with researchers, attending many inspiring talks, and Im looking forward more!
